## Interpretation limits

- Both reviewers are authors, so these results are an author audit rather than independent external validation.
- The authors report that all scores and decisions were selected and filled manually. Ordinary websites and reference material were consulted; any LLM use was limited to editorial phrasing after the judgments.
- Unclear and disagreement outcomes remain unresolved; they are not counted as confirmed positives or negatives.
