# Completed author-review records

Reviewer A and Reviewer B completed their model-blinded manual reviews
separately. Each completed 300 claim scores, nine verifier-flag decisions, and
24 sampled nonflag decisions. No adjudication was performed before the reported
agreement statistics were calculated.

The anonymous artifact includes one completed record for each reviewer. Reviewer
A is provided as an anonymized workbook, and Reviewer B is provided as a
completed review PDF. These are the two records used for all reported
author-audit calculations.
